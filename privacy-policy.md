# SugarGrip Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 August 2026_

Everything you log in SugarGrip stays on your device, with one exception: to
write the app's AI notes and insights, parts of your log are sent to Google. The
app also includes Meta's advertising SDK, which sends limited technical
information about installs and app opens — never your logs. All three parts are
explained below.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter — treat logs, craving check-ins, streaks, notes, your
name, and any photos you attach — is stored **only on your device**. The app
has no accounts, no servers, and no cloud backup. We cannot see any of it, and
there is no copy of it anywhere else. The one thing that leaves your device is
described in the next section.

## AI notes and insights

SugarGrip writes you short personal notes and insights from your own log — a
note about how a day or a week went, a line for you in the middle of a craving.
They are written by Google's Gemini model, reached through Firebase AI Logic,
and writing one means sending the part of your log it draws on to Google:

- The times, kinds and amounts of the treats you logged, and any notes you
  wrote on them
- Your craving check-ins — how they started, how they ended, the "what's going
  on" tags you chose, and anything you typed in your own words
- Small copies (at most 384 pixels on the long side) of any photos attached to
  those entries
- Your first name if you gave one, your daily allowance, and a short rolling
  summary the model keeps about your habits, so notes can be more specific
  than "you logged three things"

The exact notes the app writes will change as it grows, but the shape stays
the same: only the log behind the note being written for you, sent at the
moment it is written, never in the background. Nothing else is sent. Not your
contact details, not your device or advertising identifiers, and not an
account — there isn't one. Google returns the note, your phone stores it, and
the note is what you read. Google's handling of what it receives is governed by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the
[Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).

These notes are part of the app rather than a separate feature to switch on.
If you would rather your log were never sent anywhere, the app is not for
you — and deleting it removes everything on your device.

## What leaves your device for advertising

SugarGrip includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK so we can tell whether our ads
actually lead to installs. When you install or open the app, it sends to Meta:

- That the app was installed or opened, and when
- Basic device and app information — device model, operating system version,
  language, time zone, and app version
- An anonymous, app-specific identifier generated by Meta
- Crash and error diagnostics from the SDK itself

Meta processes this to measure and improve the performance of our advertising.
Their handling of it is governed by
[Meta's Data Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

## What Meta does not receive

Meta receives **none of your SugarGrip content**. Specifically, it never
receives:

- Your treat logs, categories, notes, or amounts
- Your craving check-ins, triggers, or SOS choices
- Your photos
- Your name, your plan, your allowance, your streaks, or your progress
- Your advertising identifier (IDFA) — collection of it is switched off in the
  app, and SugarGrip never asks permission to track you across other apps

## Your choices

Because SugarGrip does not ask for tracking permission, iOS treats tracking as
denied by default and blocks Meta's tracking endpoint. Measurement runs only
through Apple's aggregated, delayed reporting.

You can limit things further:

- **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** — confirm app tracking
  requests are off
- **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising** — turn off
  personalised ads from Apple
- **Meta ad preferences** — if you have a Facebook or Instagram account, its
  ad settings control how Meta uses activity from businesses like ours

## Photos

If you attach a photo to a log, it is saved in the app's own storage on your
device. The only time a photo leaves it is as the small copy sent to Google
when an AI note covers the day that photo belongs to, as described above.
It is never uploaded anywhere else, and never to Meta.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. There is nothing
stored elsewhere for us to delete — we hold no copy of your logs and there is
no account to close. What has already been sent to Google to write a note is
held under Google's terms, linked above. Information already sent to Meta is
aggregated ad-measurement data that is not linked to you by name and cannot be
traced back to an individual on request — to limit it going forward, use the
choices above.

## Children

SugarGrip is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you would
like the matter looked into, contact us at the address below.
