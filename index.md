# SugarGrip

<p class="lede">Most sugar apps want you to quit. SugarGrip helps you stay in
control instead.</p>

You decide how much sugar fits your life. Three things keep you in that zone: a
log that takes seconds, real help the moment a craving hits, and a picture of
your own habits that gets sharper every week.

## Logging that takes seconds

One tap logs a treat. No food database, no barcodes, no grams to look up. You
count up toward a daily allowance you set yourself, instead of down from zero.

## Craving SOS

The moment a craving hits is the moment that matters. One tap opens a breath,
then three doors — ride it out, downsize it, or have it. Every door is a win.
Choosing the treat on purpose is a completely different act from reaching for it
on autopilot.

## Your patterns, from your own log

Which day is really your big one, what you actually reach for, what sets your
cravings off, and how far the intensity falls when you ride one out. It
describes; it never lectures.

## Nothing you do breaks anything

No "days clean," no red screens, no broken-streak punishment. Logging a treat
earns XP, because noticing is the behaviour worth building. Going over your
allowance earns XP too, and the streak is designed to survive a slip.

## Private by default

Everything you log stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, and
nothing is transmitted anywhere. See the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
