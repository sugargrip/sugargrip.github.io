<div class="features" markdown="1">

## Logging that takes seconds

One tap logs a treat. No food database, no barcodes, no grams to look up. You count up
toward a daily allowance you set yourself, instead of down from zero.

## Craving SOS

The moment a craving hits is the moment that matters. One tap opens a breath, then three
doors — ride it out, downsize it, or have it. Every door is a win. Choosing the treat on
purpose is a completely different act from reaching for it on autopilot.

## Your patterns, from your own log

Which day is really your big one, what you actually reach for, what sets your cravings
off, and how far the intensity falls when you ride one out. It describes; it never
lectures.

## Nothing you do breaks anything

No "days clean," no red screens, no broken-streak punishment. Logging a treat earns XP,
because noticing is the behaviour worth building. Going over your allowance earns XP too,
and the streak is designed to survive a slip.
